\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related_work}
Prior work on charging infrastructure planning typically focuses on either mobility demand coverage or electrical grid constraints in isolation. More recent studies combine transportation simulation and power-system analysis, but integrated frameworks for bidirectional charging remain limited.

\subsection{Traffic-Aware Siting}
Traffic-aware siting methods estimate charging demand from origin-destination flows, activity patterns, and route choices. These approaches improve station accessibility and reduce detours, yet they may overlook local network bottlenecks in the distribution grid.

\subsection{Grid-Aware Siting}
Grid-aware planning approaches prioritize feeder hosting capacity, voltage stability, and peak-load mitigation. While they improve technical feasibility, they can produce suboptimal user experience if travel behavior is not modeled with sufficient detail.
