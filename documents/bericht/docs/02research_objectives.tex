\section{Research Objectives}
The continuous growth of e-mobility in urban areas presents city planners and grid operators with new challenges. While technologies such as \ac{V2G} open up new possibilities, they also require precise planning of locations.
The following chapter outlines the overarching objectives of this work and breaks them down into successive phases. Technical implementation details will not be discussed yet.

\subsection{Research goals}
The goal of this research is to develop a framework that optimizes the location and validates the placement of bidirectional charging stations under realistic traffic conditions.

\subsection{Research phases}

The project is designed to span three semesters. Phases 1 and 3 are scheduled for the first semester. Phases 4 and 5 are planned for the second semester, and phases 6 and 7 are planned for the third and final semester.

Although SUMO is not a mandatory requirement for creating a realistic traffic scenario or determining the locations for the charging stations, it will be used for running the simulation itself. For that reasons the workflow should incorporate it as much into the workflow as possible, especially to ensure backwards compatibility.

\subsection{Underlying assumptions}
The following assumptions form the basis for the modeling and simulation of traffic and charging processes.

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Homogeneous Fleet Parameters}: All electric vehicles do have the same charging power and battery capacity to reduce simulation complexity. This can change depending on the progress of this project.
    \item \textbf{Data Quality}: OpenStreetMap geometries and the network data are sufficiently complete and error-free.
\end{itemize}
